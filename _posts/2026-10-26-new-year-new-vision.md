 A NEW YEAR, A CLEAR VISION

**"Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it."** (Habakkuk 2:2)

A new year without a clear vision is like a ship without a compass—lots of motion, but no meaningful direction. God doesn't want you drifting through 2027; He wants you running with a vision so clear that everyone around you can see where you're headed.

Vision is more than a wish; it's a God-given picture of your future that pulls you forward. It determines what you say yes to and what you decline, how you spend your time, and who you spend it with. Without vision, people default to reacting to whatever life throws at them. With vision, you become proactive—you set the agenda for your year instead of the year setting it for you.

Take time this week to get before God and ask Him to show you what He wants this year to look like for you—spiritually, in your family, your finances, your health, and your service to others. Write it down. Break it into monthly and weekly goals. A vision that stays in your head is easily forgotten; a vision on paper (or in your notes app) becomes a daily reminder and a measuring stick for progress.

Don't enter this year the same way you entered the last one—without direction. See it, write it, and go after it with faith.

**CONFESSION**
I have clarity of vision for this year. I know where I'm going, and I know why. My steps are ordered by the Lord, and I run with patience the race that's set before me, refusing distraction and giving myself fully to what God has called me to accomplish this year, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
