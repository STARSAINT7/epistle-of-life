# THE POWER OF PRAYER

**"Pray without ceasing."** (1 Thessalonians 5:17)

As you step into this brand-new year, there's no better place to begin than o5n your knees. Prayer is not a religious ritual performed to earn God's attention—it's a living, ongoing conversation with your Father, and it's the single most powerful tool you've been given to shape the year ahead.

Many people start a new year with resolutions, but resolutions fade. What doesn't fade is a life anchored in prayer. When you pray, you're not informing God of something He doesn't know; you're aligning your heart, your words, and your expectations with His will and His Word. Prayer changes the atmosphere around you before it changes your circumstances.

This year, don't treat prayer as an emergency button you press only in crisis. Make it your first response, not your last resort. Set aside unhurried time with God—not to recite a wish list, but to listen, to worship, and to receive divine direction for the days ahead. The believer who prays consistently walks through the year with clarity, courage, and supernatural results that the prayerless simply cannot access.

Today, resolve that this will be your year of prayer—a year where you talk to God more than you talk about your problems.

**CONFESSION**
I begin this year rooted in prayer. My words are filled with faith, and my life is directed by the Spirit of God. I don't walk in anxiety over what the year holds, because I've committed every day into God's hands through prayer. I receive divine wisdom, direction, and grace for exploits, in Jesus' Name. Amen.
